What the organizations around the league pay the people who run them. U.S. Soccer and the MLS Players Association are tax-exempt, so each year they must list their officers, key staff and best-paid employees on the Form 990 they file with the IRS; those lists are below, by the calendar year the pay is for. MLS is a private company and files nothing public, so its figures are the few the press has reported.
