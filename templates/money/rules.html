Roster rules

What the rules let an MLS club spend, as the league published them each season. The salary budget is the cap: what a club's roster may count against it. No player counts more than the maximum budget charge, so a club pays above it only through its Designated Players, who count the maximum whatever they earn, or by buying a player's charge down with allocation money, which the league hands out each year. The minimums are the least a player on the senior and the reserve roster may be paid.
