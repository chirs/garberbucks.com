The minimum salary

The least MLS lets a player be paid. It sat at $24,000 for the league's first five seasons, every year the players' antitrust suit was in court, and the first collective bargaining agreement in 2004 raised it only to $28,000. In most seasons about a third of players are paid it or less, so where the floor sits says more about most players' pay than the stars do.
