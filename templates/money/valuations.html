Team valuations

What MLS clubs are worth: {{ lists }} published lists from Forbes and Sportico, from Forbes's first in 2008, and the values that expansion fees and club sales have put on clubs since 1996. The lists measure different things. Forbes values the team alone, leaving out stadiums and real estate; Sportico includes the team's real estate and related businesses, so it runs higher for clubs that own their grounds. Neither is a sale price; the fees and sales are.
